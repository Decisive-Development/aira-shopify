3.1 The stat block (shared section on homepage + kit PDP + refills PDP):

  a. "1 in 3 adults in the UAE snore nightly." — INVENTED (not in bank;
     "UAE" qualifier fabricated). REPLACE with bank line:
       "An estimated 1 in 4 adults snores most nights." + attribution
       "Johns Hopkins Medicine" (bank #6, YES-caveat).
  b. "2.4x more likely to be tired all day when your nose is blocked at
     night." — MUTATED (the bank's 2.4x is a heart-failure association,
     CONTEXT-only, must never touch product copy). REPLACE with bank #18:
       "A blocked nose is linked to fragmented sleep and daytime
        tiredness." + attribution "Young et al., 1997" — keep "linked to",
     no multiplier.
  c. "22% less air needed for the same pace when runners switched to nose
     breathing." — MATCHES bank #19 (Dallam 2018, YES-caveat) but needs
     softening + attribution. REPLACE with:
       "Trained nasal breathers moved up to a fifth less air for the same
        effort." + attribution "Dallam et al., 2018".
  Add a small source line/footnote style to the section (match how
  backed-by-science attributes; that page is the model — it passed audit).

     → "That one change can mean less snoring, deeper sleep..."

     breathing matters, from snoring to sporting performance." (fixes the
