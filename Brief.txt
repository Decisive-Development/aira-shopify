0. CONTEXT — WHY THIS BRIEF EXISTS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
The site popup currently carries TWO offers with equal weight:

  "LIMITED TIME OFFER / Free with every kit / Every kit comes with a free
   30-pair refill pouch, worth $19.99."   + a "SCRATCH TO REVEAL" card.

Three problems:
  (a) The eyebrow floats — a reader cannot tell whether "limited time" applies
      to the pouch or to whatever is under the card.
  (b) The headline sells the thing the visitor gets AUTOMATICALLY, with no
      action required. The card — the only thing that needs their email — has
      no stated value at all. The offer hierarchy is inverted.
  (c) A bare "SCRATCH TO REVEAL" reads as though it may simply re-reveal the
      pouch just described. Anticlimax risk on the one interaction we want.

  (d) STALE COPY: "every kit" contradicts the 6 Aug per-ORDER decision. The
      gift-cap brief's acceptance test is `grep rendered strings: "every kit"
      → 0`. This popup fails it today.

Tom's decisions, 12 August:
  - The free pouch stays UNCONDITIONAL. It is a launch offer to win first
    purchases; it is never gated behind an email. (Gating was considered and
    REJECTED — do not reintroduce it.)
  - The popup's job is EMAIL CAPTURE. The reveal stays a MYSTERY, with the
    existing WELCOME10 (10%) behind it.
  - Mystery applies to the AMOUNT, never the TYPE. The card must say it is
    revealing a code/offer. The word "gift" is banned here — it promises a
    physical item the reveal does not deliver.

1. FRAME 1 — PRE-SCRATCH (rewrite)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Structure, top to bottom:

  1. aira logo (unchanged, keep small)
  2. Eyebrow — REMOVE "LIMITED TIME OFFER".
       Replace with "WELCOME OFFER", or omit entirely.

All strings must be section/block settings, not hardcoded, so Tom can tune

Layout changes (same frame):

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
2. FRAME 2 — POST-SCRATCH (NEW — this frame does not exist yet)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Scratching does NOT reveal the code string. It reveals the OFFER, and the
email is the claim step. The literal code is never rendered in the DOM —
same rule as the newsletter popup (AIRA_QR_Popup_Fix_Brief_VSC.txt §1.2:
"Do NOT display any discount code in the popup"). The code is delivered by
the live Klaviyo welcome flow.

  1. Revealed offer, large:
       "10% off your first kit"
       — driven by a section setting (default "10% off your first kit") so a
       future change of discount is a setting edit, not a code edit.
  2. Value stack, three ticks, compact:
       ✓ 10% off your first kit
       ✓ Free 30-pair refill pouch, worth $19.99
       ✓ Free shipping to the UAE and Saudi Arabia
  3. Claim line:
       "Enter your email and we'll send your code."
  4. Email field + submit button.
       Button label: "Get my code".
       (Deliberate divergence from the sitewide purchase CTA "Get my kit" —
       this action is not a purchase. Do not "correct" it.)
  5. Legal/reassurance micro-line beneath:
       "One email to send it. Unsubscribe any time."

Form: same pattern as the existing popups — {% form 'customer' %},
POST /contact#site-popup, contact[email] required.
Tags: "newsletter, site-popup"  ← distinct from the QR popup's
"newsletter, qr-insert" so the two sources stay separable at person level.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
3. FRAME 3 — SUCCESS + ERRORS (reuse the proven pattern)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
  - customer_posted=true AND hash === '#site-popup' → reopen the popup
    showing the success state INSTEAD of the form:
      "Check your inbox — your code is on its way."
  - Already-subscribed emails return the same success redirect. Same success
    state, no special handling.
  - {{ form.errors }} (e.g. invalid email) renders inline in the form.
    Never fail silently.
  - A visitor who has scratched but not submitted, then reopens the popup in
    the same session, returns to FRAME 2 (offer already revealed) — not
    frame 1. Do not make them scratch twice.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
4. TRIGGERS + SUPPRESSION
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
  - This popup MUST NOT open on `?signup=1` or `#newsletter`. Those belong to
    the newsletter/QR popup and target existing customers arriving from a
    paper insert. Showing them a first-order offer is a wasted impression.
  - If it turns out both popups are the SAME component, STOP and report back
    to Tom before changing anything. Do not fork it, do not merge them.
  - Suppress when any of the following is true:
      * the visitor is already a known subscriber (Klaviyo identity cookie);
      * the popup was dismissed within the frequency window;
      * the URL carries `?signup=1` or a `utm_campaign=insert_*` value
        (QR-insert traffic — read for SUPPRESSION only; never parse UTMs for
        any other logic, per the parent brief's standing rule);
      * on checkout.
  - Trigger defaults, all exposed as section settings:
      delay 15s OR 40% scroll, whichever first; once per visitor per 14 days;
      desktop exit-intent OFF by default.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
5. SCRATCH MECHANIC — MOBILE + ACCESSIBILITY
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
  - Lock page scroll while a pointer is dragging on the card
    (touch-action: none on the canvas only). Today a scratch attempt on a
    phone risks flinging the page instead.
  - Auto-complete the reveal at ~50% scratched — nobody should have to clear
    the whole card.
  - Provide a non-scratch path: a visible "Reveal" button appears after ~3s,
    and the card is keyboard-operable (Enter/Space reveals). Honour
    prefers-reduced-motion by skipping the scratch animation entirely.
  - The mechanic is progressive enhancement: if canvas fails or JS errors,
    frame 2 must still render. Never trap the offer behind a broken canvas.
